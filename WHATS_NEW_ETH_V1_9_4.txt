BIGBET ETH Live Studio V1.9.4  (engine RE5.5)          built 2026-11-18

WHAT CHANGED - the bot now follows the market mood (option B)
  Before each 15-minute market it measures how jumpy ETH was in the PREVIOUS market (the same number as the light):
    LOW / MEDIUM (green)  -> calm rules: Early-Hold needs lead 1.5x (was 2.0x), steady filter 85c (was 90c)
    HIGH (red)            -> the bot makes NO new entries in this market
    no data yet           -> the V1.9.2 rules exactly (2.0x, 90c) - e.g. the first market after starting
  Stops, sizing, entry prices 90-99c, the other checkpoints, daily limits and live arming are unchanged.
  An open trade is still managed normally even when the next market is HIGH.

WHY
  Simulation on 455 recorded ETH markets at $10:
    V1.9.2:  170 trades, 2 losing, +$27.26, worst day -$2.70
    V1.9.4:  155 trades, 1 losing, +$35.37, worst day +$1.03   (better in both halves of the data)
  Nov 17-18 night: 16 -> 18 trades, +$2.38 -> +$3.44.

WHAT YOU WILL SEE IN THE LOG
  MOOD_REGIME ticker=... previous market 0.031% per minute -> CALM: calm rules ...
  SKIP_AFTER_HIGH_MOOD ticker=... : previous market was HIGH ...; no entry
  Keep the app open: the engine also reads the app's mood samples, so the regime is known right away.

TESTS: 281 tests, all pass (19 new in TEST_ETH_V1_9_4.py, including the real engine loop in calm / HIGH / no-data markets).
