Start exactly like before:  python .\BIGBET_LIVE_STUDIO_V12.py
Same data folder and settings. Trading rules UNCHANGED from V1.9.2 (no trading file changed).

NEW: MARKET MOOD light (above the market table). Display only - it never blocks or places a trade.
  * While the app is open (session running or not) it reads Kalshi's ETH index every 10 seconds
    (read-only, uses your key) and keeps 3 hours of prices in mood_samples.json in your data folder.
  * At the start of each 15-minute market it rates the PREVIOUS market by how much ETH moved per
    minute, using levels from 359 recorded ETH markets:
        LOW    under 0.045% per minute     green  "OK to trade"
        MEDIUM 0.045% - 0.072%             green  "OK to trade"
        HIGH   0.072% and up               RED    "stay away from the next market"
  * It also shows the current market so far (turns yellow if it is turning HIGH) and the last hour.
  * If the previous market has no data (app was closed), it judges the current market instead.

WHY HIGH = stay away (V1.9.2 rules, 154 simulated trades at $10):

  Weakest after HIGH in both halves of the data. Skipping trades after HIGH: 113 trades, 1 losing, +$22.73
  (vs 154 trades, 2 losing, +$24.88).
  Nov 14 evening: the light was RED (HIGH) at both losing entries (6:40 PM and 7:09 PM).
